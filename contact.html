Frequently asked questions

Does EcoAware provide official council advice?

No. EcoAware is an educational student project. For current collection rules, consult your local council.

Are recycling centre results real?

No. The locator currently demonstrates postcode validation and displays fictional examples only.

Where does my pledge go?

It stays in your current browser's localStorage until you clear it or browser data.

Does the contact form send an email?

No. The form demonstrates front-end validation only and does not transmit your message.

RECYCLING MADE CLEAR

What can I recycle?

Select a material to see practical tips. Always confirm the rules for your local council.

ℹ️ Collection rules differ by area. The guidance below is general and not a substitute for local council instructions.
🥤 Plastic Open guide +

Many areas accept empty plastic bottles, pots, tubs and trays, but acceptance varies.

  • Empty containers and rinse if needed.
  • Replace lids if your council advises.
  • Check film, bags and black plastic locally.
📦 Paper & cardboard Open guide +

Clean paper, newspapers and cardboard are commonly accepted.

  • Flatten boxes to save space.
  • Keep paper dry and free from food.
  • Greasy or coated packaging may need special handling.
🍾 Glass Open guide +

Glass bottles and jars are widely collected, sometimes separately.

  • Empty and lightly rinse bottles and jars.
  • Check whether lids should stay on.
  • Drinking glasses, mirrors and ceramics usually require different disposal.
🥫 Metal Open guide +

Food tins, drink cans and some clean foil are often recyclable.

  • Empty food and drink containers.
  • Check aerosol rules before disposal.
  • Keep batteries out of household recycling bins.
🔌 Electronics Open guide +

Electrical items should usually go to a designated collection or take-back point.

  • Remove personal data from devices.
  • Check retailer take-back options.
  • Never place loose batteries in ordinary household recycling.
🔋 Batteries Open guide +

Use approved battery collection points or retailer collection schemes.

  • Do not put batteries in household bins.
  • Follow local guidance for damaged batteries.
  • Ask an adult or specialist service for unsafe items.

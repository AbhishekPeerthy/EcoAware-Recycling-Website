SMALL STEPS, BIG DIFFERENCE

Simple habits for less waste.

Try one practical idea today and build from there.

🛍️

Bring a reusable bag

Keep a foldable bag with you for shopping trips.

💧

Refill your bottle

Carry a reusable water bottle instead of buying single-use bottles.

🥕

Plan meals

Check what you already have and reduce food waste.

📦

Reuse packaging

Repurpose sturdy boxes and containers before recycling them.

🧵

Repair and donate

Mend usable items or pass them on to others.

🍂

Compost where suitable

Check local options for food and garden waste collection.

📋

Know your local rules

Look up council guidance before putting uncertain items in recycling.

🔋

Separate batteries

Use battery collection points rather than household bins.

🧼

Empty containers

Follow local advice for emptying or rinsing recyclable packaging.

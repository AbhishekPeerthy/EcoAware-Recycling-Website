OUR MISSION

Better recycling begins with understanding.

EcoAware is a student-built educational website designed to make sustainable choices easier to understand.

Why recycle?

When materials are collected and processed appropriately, recycling can reduce the need for new raw materials and help keep useful resources in circulation.

Prevention and reuse are often even better first steps. That is why we encourage reducing waste, reusing items, repairing where practical and following local recycling guidance.

Our SDG connection

UN Sustainable Development Goal 11 promotes sustainable cities and communities. EcoAware contributes through community education about responsible waste habits.

EcoAware is an educational demonstration, not an official council waste service.

Three simple principles

🛍️

Reduce

Avoid unnecessary packaging and single-use items when possible.

🔁

Reuse

Repair, refill, share or donate items that still have life in them.

♻️

Recycle

Sort accepted materials correctly and follow your council's instructions.
